---
title: "Kornilov wins Golden Helix Abstract Challenge"
tags: award
---

Sergey Kornilov (PhD, 2014) has won the Golden Helix Abstract Challenge with an abstract based on part of his dissertation. From the [Golden Helix website](http://blog.goldenhelix.com/?p=3680):

> "Our first place winner is Dr. Sergey Kornilov, a Postdoctoral Associate in the Child Study Center at Yale University's School of Medicine. Kornilov is part of the EGLab which performs research and clinical services focused on behavioral and molecular genetics. His submission focused on the genetic basis of developmental language disorder in a geographically isolated Russian-speaking population. Kornilov will present his work to the Golden Helix community in October and will receive a new Dell laptop as well as a free license of both [SVS](http://goldenhelix.com/SNP_Variation/index.html) and [VarSeq](http://goldenhelix.com/VarSeq/index.html)."
