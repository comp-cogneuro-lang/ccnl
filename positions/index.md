# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-user-plus" %}Positions

**PhD students.** The lab admits new PhD students most years through the UConn [Language & Cognition PhD program](https://langcog.psychology.uconn.edu); applications are due December 1. Before applying, please email [Jim Magnuson](mailto:james.magnuson@uconn.edu) to discuss interests and openings.

**Postdocs and technicians.** We do not currently have any full-time or part-time openings for postdocs or technicians.

**Undergraduates.** We can almost always add undergraduates to our team via independent study or supervised research credits. Undergrads typically commit at least 9 hours per week to lab work. Please email [Jim Magnuson](mailto:james.magnuson@uconn.edu) if you are interested in learning more.
