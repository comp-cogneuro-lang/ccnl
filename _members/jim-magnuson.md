I am a Professor of Psychological Sciences at the University of Connecticut, where I am part of the [Language & Cognition PhD program](https://langcog.psychology.uconn.edu/). I am also Group Leader of the [Computational Neuroscience group](https://www.bcbl.eu/en/research/research-groups/computational-neuroscience) at the Basque Center on Cognition, Brain and Language ([BCBL](https://www.bcbl.eu/en)) in Donostia-San Sebastián, Spain, and an Ikerbasque Research Professor (Basque Foundation for Science).

My research uses computational, behavioral, and neurobiological approaches to study typical and atypical human language development and function. Current interests include speech perception and spoken word recognition, biological learning, machine learning, the psychology of artificial intelligence, and science communication.

At UConn, I directed the NSF-funded training programs Science of Learning & Art of Communication ([SLAC](https://slac.uconn.edu), 2017–2023) and Neurobiology of Language (IGERT, 2012–2019), and directed the Cognitive Science Shared Electrophysiology Resource Lab (CSSERL, 2008–2023). I was a Senior Research Scientist at Haskins Laboratories (2004–2023) and an Assistant Professor at Columbia University (2001–2004).

I received an AB in Linguistics from the University of Chicago (1993) and a PhD in Brain & Cognitive Sciences from the University of Rochester (2001), advised by Michael Tanenhaus and Richard Aslin.
